# Detective Foodie

## Description
This is a narrative-driven detective game set in my old neighborhood.  
You play as a detective returning to familiar streets to solve a long-forgotten case. Memories, locations, and time itself play a key role in uncovering the truth.

The game focuses on exploration, dialogue, and environmental storytelling rather than fast-paced action.

---

## Setting & Inspiration
- Old residential neighborhood with strong personal and historical ties  
- Themes: memory, guilt, time, and truth  

---

## Gameplay Features
- Story-focused detective gameplay  
- Exploration of an interconnected neighborhood  
- Dialogue-based investigation  
- Puzzle-solving and clue collection  
- Single-player experience  

---

## Controls
- **WASD** – Move  
- **Mouse** – Look  
- **E** – Interact  
- **I** – Inventory
